Install in minutes.

QuoteFlow is not signed by Apple or Microsoft, so your computer asks for a quick confirmation the first time.

macOS
  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag QuoteFlow to Applications (when updating, choose Replace).
  2. Open QuoteFlow. macOS says it was not opened: click Done (not “Move to Bin”).
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway next to “QuoteFlow was blocked”. Confirm with Touch ID or your password.
  4. On first launch set a password (at least 10 characters) and keep the recovery key somewhere safe.
Windows
  1. Right-click the downloaded .exe, choose Properties and tick Unblock at the bottom (if shown). Click Apply.
  2. Double-click the file. See Windows protected your PC? Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. In the installer choose Only for me (no administrator needed) or all users, then Next and Install.
  4. On first launch set a password (at least 10 characters) and keep the recovery key somewhere safe.

Questions? Answers.

General

What is QuoteFlow?

QuoteFlow is a desktop app for macOS and Windows for making quotes quickly. You pick or add a customer, fill in the products, and the app creates a fillable PDF quote with a barcode and your own branding.

Which computers does it run on?

On Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) and on macOS, on Macs with an Apple chip (M1 or later) as well as Intel Macs. Choose the right version when you download.

Do I need to be online?

No. Making quotes works offline. You only need internet to check for updates and to open your email if you use Outlook on the web.

Which languages can I make quotes in?

Dutch and English. The standard texts, terms and the email text can be edited per language.

Data and security

Where is my customer data stored?

Only on your own computer, in one encrypted file (QuoteFlow.qf) in your quotes folder. There is no account and no cloud. The app does not send customer data to the internet; for updates it only asks GitHub whether a newer version exists.

I forgot my password. Now what?

When you set your password you see a recovery key once. With that key you set a new password on the sign-in screen via Forgot password?. Without both the password and the recovery key the customer data cannot be recovered, so keep the key somewhere safe, away from your computer.

Are the PDFs encrypted too?

No. Quote PDFs have to be sent and opened, so they are stored normally in the quote's folder. Turn on disk encryption on your computer (FileVault on Mac, BitLocker on Windows).

Why does my computer show a warning when installing?

QuoteFlow is not signed by Apple or Microsoft, so macOS and Windows show a warning the first time. Only download the app from this page or from the releases page, and follow the installation steps above.

Using QuoteFlow

How do I send a quote?

After creating it you can open the email with recipient, subject and text filled in, through Outlook on the web or your default mail app. You add the PDF yourself by dragging it from the opened folder into the email.

Can I use my own logo, colours and texts?

Yes. QuoteFlow ships blank. Under Settings → Profile and template you set your branding (colours, background, logo, logo strip) and edit all texts. You can export this as a profile and import it on another installation, without customer data.

How does quote numbering work?

A quote number consists of a prefix you set, a location code and a sequence number. The sequence number counts up automatically. On a new computer it continues from the highest number found in your quotes folder.

What is the Reclameland plug-in?

An optional browser button that puts a product you configured on reclameland.nl into your quote with one click. It is off by default and you do not have to install it. You can download it under Settings → Plug-ins. It is lightweight: it only runs when you click the button and does not run in the background or slow down your browser.

Installing and updating

How do I move to a new computer?

Install QuoteFlow, choose Other folder… on the start screen and select your old quotes folder. After entering your password your customers, settings and quotes are back.

How do I update to a new version?

The app tells you when a new version is available (you can also check manually under Settings → Updates). On Windows the installer starts; on a Mac you drag the new app to Applications and choose Replace. Your data is kept.
